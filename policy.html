Privacy Policy

ScannerView · Last updated: 24 October 2026

OneSoul (“we”, “us” or “our”) provides the ScannerView mobile application (“ScannerView” or the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed or handled when you use the App.

1. No account required

ScannerView does not require you to create an account or sign in to use its core features.

2. Documents, photos and files

When you choose to scan a document or select a photo or file, the App needs access to the item you select and may use the relevant Android system picker or scanner functionality. The App is designed to process selected documents and files on your device. OneSoul does not operate a personal cloud-storage service for your documents and does not intentionally upload your scanned documents to OneSoul servers.

Files you create or export are saved to a location you choose or to a location managed by Android. You control whether you share or send a file using another app. If you share a file with another service, that service’s privacy terms apply.

3. Advertising and third-party SDKs

The App may display advertisements using Google AdMob / the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The advertising SDK may automatically collect or receive information such as an IP address, advertising or other device identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Google and its advertising partners may use this information for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. The exact information processed can depend on SDK version, settings, your region, and whether ads are personalized.

Third-party services handle information under their own terms and privacy policies. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

4. Information we do not intentionally collect

OneSoul does not ask you to provide an account, profile, or email address to use the App. We do not intentionally collect or store the contents of your scanned documents on our own servers. This does not cover information that may be processed by third-party SDKs or services described in this policy.

5. Permissions

The App may request access to the camera, photos, videos, or files when you use a feature that needs them. Access should be limited to the feature you choose. You can manage app permissions in Android settings. Available permissions may vary by Android and App version.

6. Data retention and deletion

OneSoul does not operate a cloud account or document-storage service for your files and does not maintain a server-side collection of your scanned documents to delete. Files saved on your device can be deleted by you using your device’s file manager or the relevant app. Data handled by third-party advertising or platform services is subject to those providers’ retention and deletion practices.

7. Children’s privacy

ScannerView is not specifically designed to target children. If you intend to make the App available to children or target children, review and comply with applicable Google Play Families requirements and advertising rules before release.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps when developing and maintaining the App, but no software or data transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device secure and be careful when sharing exported files.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email onesoul.support@gmail.com.
