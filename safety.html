Data Safety

Last updated: October 24, 2026

ScannerView is a document scanning and PDF utility app developed by OneSoul. We designed the app to help users scan documents and manage their documents directly on their devices, without requiring an account or a OneSoul-operated cloud storage service.

1. Data Collection by ScannerView

ScannerView does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its core document-scanning features.

ScannerView does not operate its own cloud server to collect, upload, or store users' scanned documents or document contents.

Documents scanned or processed using ScannerView are handled locally on the user's device, provided the user does not choose to share or export them through another application or service.

2. Camera Permission

ScannerView may request camera permission when you choose to scan a document using your device's camera.

The camera is used to capture document images for scanning. Document scanning and processing take place on your device. ScannerView does not send camera captures or scanned document contents to OneSoul-operated cloud servers.

You can manage or revoke camera permission through your device settings. Camera-based scanning may not work if permission is denied.

3. Files and Documents

When you select a document to import or process, ScannerView accesses the selected file to provide the requested functionality.

Documents processed locally by ScannerView are not uploaded to OneSoul-operated cloud servers. ScannerView does not automatically upload your documents to a OneSoul cloud account.

If you choose to share or export a document using another app or service, that transfer is handled by the destination app or service and is subject to its own privacy practices.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

ScannerView may display advertisements using Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

Although ScannerView does not operate a cloud server for collecting users' documents, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share certain information to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, maintain security, and prevent advertising fraud.

Depending on the SDK configuration and applicable settings, this information may include:

  • IP address
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • Interactions with advertisements and the app
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be processed by Google and its relevant advertising partners in accordance with their applicable privacy policies and settings.

ScannerView does not use its own document-storage server to collect users' scanned document contents for advertising.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

5. Data Sharing

OneSoul does not operate a cloud service that receives or stores users' scanned documents.

However, information may be transmitted to Google or other relevant third parties through the advertising SDK or other services included in the app, where applicable. Such processing is subject to the relevant service providers' practices.

If you deliberately share a document through another application, that application may receive the document you selected.

6. Data Storage and Security

ScannerView is designed to process documents locally on your device rather than storing them on OneSoul-operated cloud servers.

Documents saved by the app may remain in the app's storage or in a location selected by you, depending on the feature you use. You can manage saved documents using the app's available controls and your device's file-management features.

Data handled by third-party services, including advertising services, is subject to those providers' applicable security and retention practices.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

ScannerView does not maintain a OneSoul-operated cloud database of users' scanned documents.

Documents saved locally may remain on your device until you delete them. Removing a document from ScannerView may not remove a separate copy that you previously exported or shared elsewhere.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is subject to the relevant providers' retention and deletion policies.

8. Children's Privacy

ScannerView is not specifically designed to collect children's personal information. The app's advertising configuration and availability are subject to applicable laws and Google Play policies.

9. Changes to This Data Safety Page

We may update this page if ScannerView's features, integrations, or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
