Contact & Support

ScannerView · OneSoul

Need help with document scanning, PDF conversion, exporting a file, or reporting a problem? Contact support by email.

Email ScannerView Support

Email: onesoul.support@gmail.com

What to include in your email

  • A short description of the problem.
  • Your Android version and ScannerView app version, if known.
  • The steps that caused the issue and any error message shown.

Please do not email sensitive documents, identity documents, passwords, payment details, or other private information unless genuinely necessary. If you attach a screenshot, check it first and hide personal information.

Privacy

We use the information you choose to include in your email to respond to your support request. Your email is handled through your email provider and our support mailbox; their respective privacy practices apply.
